# Changeover cut by 15 minutes

Every filler changeover 15 minutes shorter: can to bottle from 60 to 45 minutes, product changes from 30 to 15. The kind of gain a quick-changeover (SMED) workshop aims at. Nothing else changes from [[Baseline]].
