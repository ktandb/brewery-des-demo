# Second filler

A second filler, with its own labeller and packer and palletiser so it can actually run (a filler on its own would just wait for the first line's labeller). Same speeds, shifts and breakdown rates as the first line. Everything upstream stays as in [[Baseline]].

Why ask: the filler is the most visible machine in the plant and the one that stops most often. If the brewery is short of packaging capacity, this should show it.
